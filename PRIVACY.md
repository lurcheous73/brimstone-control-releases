# Brimstone Control privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Brimstone Control is a client for a Brimstone Core that you operate. The app is available on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. This policy explains the information used by Control, its optional services, and its offline demo.

## Your Core and local data

When you connect Control to your Core, the app sends the address, sign-in details, library and playback requests, and playback state needed to run the features you choose. These requests go to the Core you configure. Brimstone Cottage does not host customer Cores and does not automatically receive your Core credentials, library, listening history or media files from Control. Your Core's operator controls its storage and logs.

Control stores settings and temporary media data on your device. It stores authentication material in the device Keychain where the platform provides one. On-device storage follows the platform's storage and backup behaviour; data held by your Core is managed on that Core.

The optional offline demo uses bundled, original sample audio and demonstration metadata. It does not need a Core account or send demo library activity to a Core.

## Optional services

If you enable the built-in Tailscale connection, Tailscale processes account, device, network and connection metadata to provide remote connectivity. This may include an account identifier, device name, IP address, operating system, node key, connection times and operational logs. Tailscale's [privacy policy](https://tailscale.com/legal/privacy-policy) describes its handling and retention. The contents of your Core traffic remain encrypted in transit through Tailscale.

If you choose a music provider or streaming source, Control or your Core may contact that provider to authenticate, find content or play it. The provider's privacy terms apply to that use. Apple Music uses Apple's MusicKit permissions and your Apple account. Other optional sources and web links are used only when you select or configure them.

Control may request local network, media library, camera or microphone access for features that need them. The system asks for permission where required. You can change these permissions in device settings.

## Information sent to Brimstone Cottage

Control has no advertising or cross-app tracking. Brimstone Cottage does not operate an in-app analytics service for Control. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and diagnostic details. We use it to answer and resolve your request. Do not send passwords or private media in support messages.

## Your choices

You can use the offline demo without connecting a Core. You can disconnect a Core, leave optional providers and Tailscale unused, change system permissions, or remove Control. To delete data stored on your customer-operated Core, use that Core's controls or contact its operator. For support information you sent to us, email [legal@brimstonecottage.uk](mailto:legal@brimstonecottage.uk) to request access or deletion, subject to applicable record-keeping duties.

Questions about this policy: [legal@brimstonecottage.uk](mailto:legal@brimstonecottage.uk). We will update the effective date when this policy changes.

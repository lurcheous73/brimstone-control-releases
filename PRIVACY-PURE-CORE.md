# Brimstone Pure Core privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026

Brimstone Pure Core is a Mac app for a personal music library and local playback. This policy covers the Mac app and the Core it starts on your Mac.

## Music and device data kept locally

Pure reads music in its default music folder and folders you add. It stores library details, artwork, playlists, queue and playback state in its local database. It also stores settings and the credentials and identifiers needed to secure its local Core and pair compatible Control devices. Audio files and MiniDisc recordings stay on your Mac unless you deliberately send or export them to a device you control. A paired device on your local network can request library and playback information from your Core.

Brimstone Cottage does not operate a cloud account or remote library for Pure and does not automatically receive your music files, library, listening history or Core credentials. Pure has no advertising or cross-app tracking, and Brimstone Cottage does not operate an in-app analytics service for it.

## Music information

Pure reads tags and artwork from the music files you choose and keeps its library on your Mac. The Mac App Store release does not automatically send your artist, album, track, CD or Now Playing details to online metadata, artwork or lyrics services. You can still open external links in your browser, or add a music file or artwork yourself.

## Optional Apple Music

If you enable Apple Music and grant permission through macOS, Pure uses MusicKit to request access to the Apple Music information needed for that feature. Apple handles that information under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can withdraw access in macOS settings and leave Apple Music disabled in Pure.

## Your choices and support

You can manage Pure's music sources, pair a compatible device in the app, change macOS permissions, and remove the app's local data. Unpair a Control device to revoke its access. Turn off Apple Music in Pure or revoke its macOS permission to stop that optional connection.

If you contact Brimstone Cottage for support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and diagnostic details. We use it to answer and resolve your request. Do not send passwords or private media in support messages. To ask about support information or request access or deletion, email [legal@brimstonecottage.uk](mailto:legal@brimstonecottage.uk), subject to applicable record-keeping duties.

We will update the effective date when this policy changes.
